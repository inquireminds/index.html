School Routines & Classroom Expectations

Building habits for academic and personal success in intermediate school.

Locker Access Routine

  • Entry & Recess Times: Lockers are visited upon morning arrival, before recess, after recess, and at dismissal.
  • In-Class Focus: Backpacks stay in lockers during class time to keep learning spaces safe and organized.

Prepared to Learn Daily

  • Basic Supplies: Pencils, eraser, ruler, calculator, lined notebook, and recharged TDSB device/Chromebook.
  • Class Readiness: Arriving on time with all materials ready before the bell rings.

Timely Work & Responsibility

  • Deadlines: Assignments must be completed and submitted on time to maintain steady progress.
  • Proactive Communication: If extra time or support is needed, students are expected to talk to the teacher before the due date.
